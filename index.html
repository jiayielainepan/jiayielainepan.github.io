Hi! My name is Elaine, and I am currently an M.S.E. student in Industrial and Operations Engineering at the University of Michigan and a Research Fellow in the Section of Digitally Enabled Care at NYU Grossman School of Medicine. At NYU, I work with Dr. Angela Mastrianni and Dr. Elizabeth Stevens on evaluating patient-facing AI tools, including an LLM-as-a-judge system for a GLP-1 medication chatbot.

I earned my B.S.E. in Industrial and Operations Engineering from Michigan in May 2026, graduating summa cum laude. As an undergraduate, I worked at the Center for Healthcare Engineering and Patient Safety (CHEPS) with Dr. Amy Cohn, forecasting staffing demand for state psychiatric hospitals, and studied provider workload in the NICU at C.S. Mott Children’s Hospital. My interests are in operations research, human factors, and AI evaluation for healthcare. When I’m not working, I’m usually dancing, planning my next trip, or taking far too many photos of my food!
